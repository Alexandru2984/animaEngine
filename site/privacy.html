Privacy

The app collects nothing. animaEngine makes no network connection of any kind: no telemetry, no crash reporting, no update checks, no accounts, no advertising. Nothing about you, your computer or what is on your screen leaves it.

What the app keeps, on your computer

  • Its settings and your scenes, in your user's configuration folder (~/.config/animaengine on Linux, %APPDATA%\animaEngine on Windows).
  • The pictures you add, in its asset library, and decoded copies of them in a cache folder, so they load faster.
  • After a crash, a copy of the scene from just before it, for anima-engine --recover.

All of it stays on your computer, in your own user's folders. Uninstalling leaves it in place for a later install; delete those folders to remove it. The FAQ says where they are.

The diagnostic report

Appearance → About → Copy diagnostic report puts a short text on your clipboard: the app's version, your system's name, your graphics card and the app's latest warnings — never the paths of your files. The app sends it nowhere. It goes only where you paste it.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or scripts, and it loads nothing from other sites. It is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, receives your IP address with each request; see GitHub's privacy statement. Downloads come from GitHub's release pages, under the same statement.
