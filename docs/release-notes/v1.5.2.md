# animaEngine 1.5.2

A fix for sway, Hyprland and the other wlroots compositors, where 1.5.1
would not quit, and one for the first-run tour.

## Fixed

- **On sway, Hyprland and other wlroots compositors (native Wayland),
  Quit did not quit.** Quit in the tray, the Quit shortcut, and SIGTERM
  (what `kill` and a session manager send) all made the overlay again
  instead of ending the app, as if the compositor had closed it. A quit
  ends it again, after saving.
  The overlay is still made again when the compositor closes it, as when
  you unplug its monitor. Other desktops were not affected.
- **The first-run tour no longer points at a hidden ⚙ button.** With
  Appearance → *Show the ⚙ button* off, its first step told you to click
  the button in the top-right corner. It names the edit-mode shortcut as
  you have it bound, the tray, and starting animaEngine again.

## Under the hood

The native Wayland loop and the X11 frame are split into small steps,
and the settings panel takes the whole configuration. Nothing you use
changes; it makes the next fixes easier to get right.
