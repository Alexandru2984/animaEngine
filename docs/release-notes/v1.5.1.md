# animaEngine 1.5.1

Animated PNGs, a way to hide the ⚙ button, and two fixes: for people
coming from 1.2.0, and for sway and Hyprland.

## What you'll notice

- **Animated PNG (APNG) plays.** Drop one in and it moves the way a GIF
  does, every frame for as long as it asks. One you added under 1.5.0,
  which stood still, plays now too.
- **The ⚙ button can be hidden.** Appearance → *Show the ⚙ button*. With
  it off, its corner passes clicks like the rest of the overlay. Open
  edit mode with its shortcut, from the tray, or by starting animaEngine
  again from your apps — that always works, whatever your desktop has.
  In edit mode the button is there as the way out.

## Fixed

- **Coming from 1.2.0, the characters looked as they did then**, with no
  walk, fall or sleep: 1.2.0 left its pictures in the folder it was
  started from, usually your home folder, and 1.5.0 found those first.
  The ones 1.5 draws are used now. The old folder, `assets/demo` in your
  home folder, is left alone; you may delete it.
- **On sway, Hyprland and other wlroots compositors (native Wayland), the
  app quit when its monitor went** — unplugging the monitor it was on, or
  on Hyprland starting it from `exec-once` before the monitors were up.
  It moves to a monitor that is there now, and waits while there is none.
