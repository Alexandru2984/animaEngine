# Getting help

- **[Questions and known problems](docs/faq.md)** — settings, shortcuts,
  the GPU, Windows' warning, removing the app.
- **Something does not work?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/Alexandru2984/animaEngine/issues/new/choose)
  with the diagnostic report: Appearance → About → **Copy diagnostic
  report**, then paste it. It holds the version, your system, your
  graphics card and the app's latest warnings, never your files' paths;
  the app sends nothing itself.
- **A security problem?** Privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.
